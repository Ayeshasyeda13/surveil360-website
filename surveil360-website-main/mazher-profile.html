An entrepreneur and business leader with more than two decades of experience across travel and tourism, BPO and data processing, surveillance technology, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and enterprise solutions.

// Journey

1996 – 1998

Graduated from Osmania University (1996) and completed the IATA/UFTAA programme through the IATA Learning Center, Geneva, Switzerland (1998).

1999

Established his own air travel and tourism business, serving VIPs, corporate and general customers with travel connecting India to destinations worldwide.

2014

Entered the BPO and data-processing sector, founding BaseCamp Data Solutions as a partnership firm (Firm Registration No. 3092 of 2014) with a mission to build a sustainable business and create jobs and professional skills among young people.

2014 – 2020

Built a dedicated surveillance and data-processing center in Hyderabad, growing from about 10 employees to more than 250 personnel across remote surveillance, video monitoring, data processing, workforce management and enterprise security operations.

2020 – 2022

Upskilled through learning and certifications in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Facial Recognition Technology, Cybersecurity, and emerging cyber threats.

2023

Transitioned the business into BASECAM Data Solutions Private Limited, creating a stronger corporate structure for national and international growth.

2026

Advanced his AI knowledge further with a Mastermind AI certification. BASECAM has also established BASECAM Data Solutions Company in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

People First
